Quietlane Support

No Quietlane account is required. Use the local focus, sleep and settings tools immediately. Social platforms may require their own sign-in for private content.

Need help?

Email nishaadbharaswadkar@gmail.com. Include your phone model, OS version, Quietlane version and a short description. Do not send passwords, authentication codes or private messages.

A website is blank or won’t sign in

Check your internet connection and try Reload. Instagram also has a Sign in recovery action. Some websites restrict embedded browsers; Quietlane cannot bypass those restrictions.

What gets blocked?

Quietlane filters known distracting content inside its own browser. It does not modify or block native apps installed on your phone.
