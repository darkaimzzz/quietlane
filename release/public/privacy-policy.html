Quietlane Privacy Policy

No Quietlane account

Quietlane does not require registration, an email address, or a subscription. Focus sessions, sleep schedules, settings and local activity work without an app account.

Data on your device

Quietlane stores preferences, schedules, timer state and in-app browsing durations in local app storage. Up to 90 days of activity are kept. Quietlane has no analytics service, advertising SDK, account server or telemetry backend. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to its settings.

Social websites

Opening a social platform loads that platform's real website in a native browser. The platform receives normal web requests, including your IP address, and may set cookies, collect data, display ads, or require its own account. Those platform accounts are optional to Quietlane's local tools. Quietlane cannot provide private messages without the platform's sign-in.

Filtering and sign-in

Filtering code checks page links and selected page elements to hide distracting features. It does not extract or transmit passwords, message contents, or login cookies. Website sessions are managed by the system WebView. Filtering applies only inside Quietlane and can change when third-party websites change.

Sharing and deletion

Export shares your settings and local activity only when you request it through the system share sheet. Reset clears Quietlane preferences and activity, not website login cookies. Sign out within each website to end its session. Removing Quietlane removes its local data; backups are managed by your operating system.

Permissions

Quietlane's own tools do not request camera or microphone access. Live camera/microphone capture is disabled in the embedded browser, and Android capture permissions are blocked. Existing-file uploads may be available through the system picker. Focus and schedule tools remain available without media permissions.
